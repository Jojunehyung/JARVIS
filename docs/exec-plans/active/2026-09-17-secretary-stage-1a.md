## Goal

Stage 1-A of the "secretary" direction for the `업무` / `미팅` tabs. Four things: (1) a meeting's follow-ups become **structured items** (`meetings[].followUps[]`) with an owner flag, an optional due date and a done state, next to the free-text `후속 조치` that stays; an item the user marks `내 담당` is **registered automatically as a work item** and the two stay linked both ways; (2) undone work items are **carried into today's list** by derivation — nothing is moved or rewritten, the `지난 미완료` section and `오늘로 옮기기` go away; (3) a **meeting-prep card** at the top of the `업무` tab for every schedule event today or tomorrow that belongs to a project — the last meeting's decisions, open follow-ups, progress and linked tasks, one tap from its minutes; (4) the daily briefing states the prep count, overdue follow-ups and carried work as **numbers**. Everything is a record or a derivation: nothing here pays, promotes, completes a task or reaches `computeGrades` / `krProgress` / `todoOf`.

## Decisions the user made (final — record, do not reopen)

1. **Follow-up items.** `meetings[].followUps: [{ id, text, mine: boolean, due?: "YYYY-MM-DD", done: boolean, workId?: string }]`. A new item defaults `mine: false` — the user turns `내 담당` on. Text cap 200 chars (`MEETING_LIMITS.followUp`), 30 items per meeting (`MEETING_FOLLOWUPS_MAX`).
2. **Automatic work registration.** A follow-up with `mine: true` is registered as a work item: `date` = the meeting's date when that is today or later, otherwise today; `source: "meeting"` (a third source value beside `manual` / `ai`; lead chip `회의`); linked both ways — `followUps[].workId` on the meeting, and on the work item `link: { kind: "meeting", id: <meetingId>, followUpId: <followUp.id> }` (`WORK_LINK_KINDS` unchanged; `followUpId` is an extra key on the existing `meeting` link kind, read only by the mirror logic — `workLinkLabel` keeps reading `kind` / `id`). Turning `mine` on later creates the item then; turning it off removes the linked work item **only if it is still undone**. Completing the work item marks the follow-up done and vice versa — **done state only**; text, due and date never mirror. Deleting a work item leaves the follow-up on the meeting, unlinked (`workId` cleared, `mine` untouched).
3. **Events get an optional `projectId`.** `프로젝트 (선택)` picker in `EventModal`, listing `meetingProjects`. The prep card also matches by title: an event with no `projectId` whose trimmed title equals a previous meeting's trimmed title counts as that meeting's project.
4. **Carry-forward is derived, never stored.** `workOn(state, today, today)` includes every undone item dated before today; its lead chip reads `이월 {n}일` (`n` = days since its own date); carried items sort oldest first, above today's items. The `지난 미완료` section, `workPastOpen`, `onMove`, `moveWorkToToday` and `오늘로 옮기기` are deleted. Paging to another day shows only that day's items, as now. Select mode and bulk delete stay and cover the carried rows.

## Storage arithmetic (goes into the meetings-region comment, the work-region comment, `meetings.md` and `daily-work.md`)

Unit: string length against `STORAGE_BUDGET` = 3,672,064 chars, as in the meetings region.

- Every meeting record grows by `,"followUps":[]` = **15 chars** (v26 backfill).
- One follow-up: `{"id":"xxxxxxxxxx","text":"","mine":false,"done":false}` ≈ **50 chars** of overhead + its text (+ 1 comma between items); `,"due":"YYYY-MM-DD"` adds 19 and `,"workId":"xxxxxxxxxx"` adds 22, so a fully-keyed item is ≈ **92 chars + text**. Typical 40-char item without due ≈ 90; with due and `workId` ≈ 132. A full 200-char item with both keys ≈ 292; thirty full items ≈ **8,760 chars**.
- A completely full meeting (ten task links, thirty full progress entries — 17,050 in `meetings.md`) with thirty full follow-ups ≈ 17,050 + 15 + 8,760 ≈ **25,825 chars**. Typical minutes (~850) + three typical progress entries (~375) + 31 + 15 + four typical follow-ups (~4 × 130 = 520) ≈ **1,810 chars**; three a working day ≈ 1.36 M chars a year (**37 %** of the budget a year, about **2 years 8 months** before `recordFits` refuses).
- Each `mine` follow-up also creates a work item: the v25 overhead (~100) + title (≤ 60) + `,"link":{"kind":"meeting","id":"xxxxxxxxxx","followUpId":"xxxxxxxxxx"}` ≈ 66 ≈ **~210 chars** for a 40-char text; when the text exceeds 60 chars the full text is copied into `note` (≤ 200), so the largest such item ≈ 100 + 60 + 210 + 66 ≈ **436 chars**. `recordFits` measures the meeting record **plus every work item the reconcile creates** before either is written.
- Events: `,"projectId":"xxxxxxxxxx"` = **25 chars** per linked event, only when chosen.
- Carry-forward adds nothing: no field is written; the list is filtered at render.
