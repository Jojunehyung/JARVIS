# Home becomes one quantitative CV; the growth tab is removed
- Status: completed
- Date: 2026-09-15
- Needs approval: **no** — no `CERTS` / `EXAMS` / `WEIGHT_MATRIX` / `CERT_W_EXC` row, no `migrate` block and no `v` bump, no `liferpg-*` key, no `store` call site, and no user data deleted: every record the removed surfaces showed stays in the save and stays reachable (decision D8 below). - Agents: planner → implementer → cleanup → verifier → docs-syncer

## Goal
The user asked, verbatim: `성장탭을 없애주고, 홈화면에 정량적 평가만 남겨줘` / `복잡한거 다 지우자` / `홈화면을 한개의 CV로 만들고, 밑에 달성하고자 하는것에 대한 %만 조그맣게 따로 만들어줘`. In product terms: remove the `성장` tab, strip home down to one CV that evaluates the user in numbers and grades, and put one small percentage under it.

Decisions the user made when asked are final and are not re-opened here: (1) the figure under the CV is role-model proximity only (`목표별 진행률은 목표탭에서만 하고 롤모델 근접도만`) — goal progress stays in `목표`; (2) promotion moves onto the CV's grade rows, which open `PromoteModal` exactly as the growth rows do today; (3) role model, backup and reset go behind a small settings button in a corner of home; (4) the `오늘 브리핑` card and the `오늘 할 일` list leave home, the briefing still auto-opens once a day, and journal and weekly review stay reachable from it.

Outcome: five tabs (`홈 · 목표 · 실행 · 일정 · 사업`); home is one CV card plus one proximity line and fits a single 390 × 844 screen on the demo save; nothing the growth tab or the home cards showed becomes unreachable.

### Decisions (already made — implement them, do not re-decide)

**D1 — What "quantitative evaluation only" admits.** A home row is admitted when its value is a number, a grade or an ordinal credential level: age, degree level with status, months of practice, area grade `n/9`, certification D, exam band with D, counts (held certifications, exam bests, portfolio pieces, trophies, verified achievements), and role-model proximity %. A nominal field appears only as the label that qualifies such a value — the major of the degree, the most recent role of the practice months, the name of the certification or exam whose D is shown. Excluded from home: school and employer names, e-mail, phone, gender, `현재 신분` (the header states it on every tab), knowledge and job directions, everything dated today (briefing counts, schedule and business lines, today's tasks, today's completion count), goal progress and pace (user decision 1), money (a record, not an evaluation), and free-text achievement entries (one tap away behind the `성취` row). Portrait and display name stay as the CV's identity.

**D2 — The CV card.** One `<section className="bg-zinc-900 border border-zinc-800 rounded-2xl p-4 space-y-3">` with three blocks in reading order (identity → records → grades; the grades sit last so they touch the proximity line computed from them):
1. **Identity row** `flex items-start gap-3`: `Portrait` at size 64 inside the existing frame (`rounded-2xl overflow-hidden bg-zinc-950 border border-zinc-700 shrink-0`); a `flex-1 min-w-0` column with `{displayName(state.profile)}` (`font-black text-base truncate`), `{ageText(state.profile, today)}` (`text-xs text-zinc-500 mt-0.5 truncate`) and the existing `프로필` button (classes unchanged, `mt-2`) → `onProfile`; last, the settings button (D5). `프로필` stays the only edit control — `ProfileModal` owns the photo, the personal facts and the records.
2. **Records**, `space-y-1`, one module-level `CvFact({ label, children, onClick })` row each (a `<button>` when `onClick` is given, else a `<div>`): `flex items-baseline gap-2 text-xs w-full text-left`, label `w-16 shrink-0 text-zinc-500`, value `min-w-0 flex-1 truncate text-zinc-300`, counts and D values `font-mono`.
