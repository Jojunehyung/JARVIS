# CERTS V1.3 — all currently issued national qualifications
- Status: completed
- Date: 2026-09-06
- Agents: (pre-harness) enumeration and calibration workflows with adversarial verification

## Goal
User request: "존재하는 전체 국가면허 자격증" — extend the certification table from 204 entries (V1.2) to every currently issued Korean national qualification (technical qualifications at all levels, professional qualifications, licences), calibrate difficulty D, assign categories and grade ladders, and keep every existing D unchanged ([Rule 6](../../design-docs/core-beliefs.md#rule-6)).

## What was done
- Enumeration across 11 segments (Q-Net technical qualifications, professional qualifications by statute, licences); 870 candidates after de-duplication against the 204 existing rows; 63 removed by rule (temporary licences, education-only issuance, generic/variant naming, mis-scoped items) → 807 new rows; teacher ladders unified per school level.
- Difficulty D by three independent lenses (pass rate & eligibility / anchor comparison / preparation & prerequisites) → median → adversarial verification per batch (band, anchor position, spread ≥ 12, ladder monotonicity); all 807 verified.
- Categories 10 → 15 (화학·재료·섬유, 농림·축산·식품, 운송·항공·해양, 교육·복지·상담, 문화·예술·디자인); jobs 16 → 21; knowledge directions 13 → 18; 80 stage-group ladders (`sg`/`st`), including joins to existing rows (사회조사분석사, 직업상담사, 유통관리사, 사회복지사).
- Job-fit: the 150 new cross cells researched per job against posting mention rates and adversarially verified → **all C**; 47 individual exceptions with statutory/posting evidence added to `CERT_W_EXC` (e.g. 사회복지사 1급 S for 교육·복지, 정신건강 전문요원 A for 보건·의료, 검수사/검량사/감정사 S for 운송·항공·해양).
- Completeness audit against the Q-Net list: 4 renames applied (웹디자인개발기능사, 피복아크용접기능사, 프로그래밍기능사, 컴퓨터시스템기사; D unchanged), 3 missing qualifications added (반려동물행동지도사 1·2급, 스마트농업관리사), 2027/2028 renames deferred, 전문의 family still excluded (D cap 100).
- `DIFF_RAW_VERSION` → "1.3"; catalog cap 40 → 60 with a remainder hint.
