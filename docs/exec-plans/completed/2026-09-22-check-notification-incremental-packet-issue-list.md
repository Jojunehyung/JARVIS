Three bundles in one plan, executed in four phases. **Phase 1** is Bundle B (the user's request today: "when asking the AI, reflect only what came after the last work refresh"). **Phase 2** is Bundle A (the `확인 필요` local notification — the user's final decisions are restated verbatim below). **Phase 3** is Bundle C (`교육` as a meeting kind, and the `이슈 목록` overview screen — the user's decisions of later today, final). **Phase 4** is docs.

## Goal
**Bundle B — the incremental work packet.** Every `오늘 업무 만들기` packet restates the ten newest meetings whole, so a user who refreshes daily pastes the same minutes every day. Registering at least one AI work proposal from the work bridge now stamps `act.workRefreshedAt` (a date, a user-action stamp like `act.briefingSeen`). When the stamp exists, the send pane offers two chips, `지난 갱신 이후` (preselected) and `전체`; in since-mode `buildWorkPacket(state, today, { since })` carries only the meetings dated or created on or after the stamp, a compact section of what changed on older meetings (progress entries dated on or after the stamp, plus every open `mine` follow-up of an older meeting — follow-ups carry no date, so all of them are listed), and the documents added on or after the stamp. Goals, open tasks, schedule, contracts and work records are current state, not history, and stay as they are. Without a stamp, the packet is byte-identical to today's.

**Bundle A — the `확인 필요` notification.** A local notification (no network, no AI — the app decides everything from its own state) that lists three facts: whether today's work was refreshed, which past project appointments (14 days) have no meeting minutes, and which work items are carried over. It refreshes on every app open and, when the app is installed, through Chrome Periodic Background Sync — re-shown by the service worker from a cached mirror of the last rendered text. Off by default; a settings switch `확인 필요 알림` turns it on after `Notification.requestPermission()`. Tapping the notification opens the `이슈 목록` screen (Bundle C), where all three of its lines live.

**Bundle C — training records and the `이슈 목록` screen.** A meeting record may be a `교육` (training) record: the same fields under different labels, the same progress, follow-ups, transcript, task links and AI flag. And one persistent overview, `이슈 목록` (`modal.type: "issues"`), lists in one place what needs attention: open work/tasks/follow-ups by track, the next 14 days of schedule, the newest training records, and each project's latest minutes with its open follow-ups — derived at render, storing nothing.

## Planner decisions (recorded here so the implementer does not re-decide)
1. **`AI 갱신 {since}` on the not-refreshed line — yes.** When `act.workRefreshedAt` exists, the `오늘 할 일 미갱신 · {which}` line ends with ` · AI 갱신 {workRefreshedAt}`. A fact the user asked to be able to see; it changes nothing about the definition (created-today items / reader seen).
2. **Head rule 3 gains its sentence in since-mode only.** The full packet (no stamp, or the `전체` chip) stays byte-identical to HEAD's output; a `workPacketHead(since)` helper returns the extended array only when `since` is set.
3. **Since-mode section order:** `## 최근 회의록 ({n}건 · {since} 이후)` → `## 이전 회의록의 새 기록` → `## 문서 ({since} 이후)` → `## 업무 기록 (이월·어제·오늘)`. The document section is a new section, not folded into the meetings.
4. **Since-mode trim order:** the existing nine reductions in their order, with two new steps inserted before the last: … (7) work records → **(8) drop `## 문서`** → **(9) drop `## 이전 회의록의 새 기록`** → (10) meetings 2 → 0. Documents go before the older-meeting section because a progress entry is closer to an action than a document summary.
5. **Hidden meetings (`aiHidden`) in since-mode:** in `## 최근 회의록` they contribute their date and title only (unchanged, through `meetingPacketLines`); in `## 이전 회의록의 새 기록` a hidden meeting contributes nothing and is not counted in the header line's `진행사항` count. Track filtering is `packetTracks(state)` in both sections and for documents, the same as every other packet section.
6. **Cache request URL is relative:** `./__check-summary`, resolved by the page and by the SW against the same scope, so a subpath deploy (`/JARVIS/`) and a root deploy both work. The task text says `/__check-summary`; the leading-slash form would miss under a subpath.
7. **The activate handler keeps the `life-check` cache.** Today's loop deletes every cache that is not the current build's; without this exception every deploy would evict the summary. The precache list and the fetch handler are untouched.
8. **`notificationclick` with an open client:** the SW focuses it and posts `{ open: "issues" }` to it; the page listens on `navigator.serviceWorker` `message` and opens the named screen. Focus alone would leave the user on whatever tab was open; `clients.openWindow("./?open=issues")` is the path when no client exists. **The notification opens `이슈 목록`, not the reader** (Bundle C, coordinator's recommendation accepted): its three lines — unrefreshed day, appointments without minutes, carried work — are all facts the issue list states, and the reader is one row away from it. The original `open: "reader"` value is kept as a second accepted value of the boot param and the SW message, so a cached notification from before this change still lands somewhere sensible.
9. **Boot param `?open=issues` | `?open=reader`** opens the named screen regardless of `act.briefingSeen`, then `history.replaceState` removes the query so a reload does not reopen it. Closing the reader stamps the day through `closeBriefing` exactly as today; closing the issue list stamps nothing.
10. **Browsers without the APIs:** when `!("Notification" in window)` or `!("serviceWorker" in navigator)` (the single-file demo, `file://`, an old WebView) the switch is disabled and the caption states `이 브라우저에서는 알림을 쓸 수 없어요`. The switch never writes `settings.checkNotify` in that case.
11. **`renotify` compares counts** `{ notRefreshed, minutes, carried }` against the cached entry's counts (read before the write), so a re-render with the same facts does not buzz the phone again; a session-level ref is not enough because the app is closed between opens.
12. **The notification is all-track.** It never leaves the device, so `packetTracks`/`workInAiOf` do not apply to it (the day-job switch is about AI packets only).
13. **`doneDates` on an appointment does not suppress the minutes-missing row** — a done appointment is exactly one that happened and should have minutes. `skip` dates are respected by `occurrencesOf` already.
