# Design
<!-- src: KD --><!-- src: SPEC-8 -->

Dark, single-theme mobile web UI (390 × 844 artboard, `max-w-md` centred column). The design's job is to make evidence and pace legible, not to motivate. Copy tone follows [Rule 13](design-docs/core-beliefs.md#rule-13): numbers and facts only.

## Typography and rhythm
Numbers, D values, P, percentages, D-day, and system labels (`LIFE MANAGER`, `PROFILE SETUP`, `RANK UP`, `ACHIEVEMENT`, `STAGE` — 2026-09-18) are `font-mono`; section captions `text-xs tracking-widest`. Body mostly `text-xs`–`text-sm`; modal titles `text-base font-bold`; overlay hero `text-2xl font-black`. Vertical rhythm `space-y-4` (sections) / `space-y-3` (inside cards). Progress bar (`Bar`): `h-2 rounded-full`, track `bg-zinc-800`, fill in the context colour, width transition 0.7 s ease-out.

## Constraints
- Tailwind v3 core utilities only, no arbitrary values; icons from lucide only (in use: Trophy, Target, Plus, X, Lock, RotateCcw, Settings, Check, Star, IdCard, ClipboardList, ListChecks, CalendarDays, MessagesSquare, Briefcase, Camera, Paperclip, Link). `Flag` (the old `홈` nav icon) went out 2026-09-16, replaced by `IdCard` for the renamed `프로필` tab; `MessagesSquare` came in the same day for the new `미팅` tab; `ListChecks` came in 2026-09-17 for the new `업무` tab.
- No game visuals ([Rule 7](design-docs/core-beliefs.md#rule-7)); grades 0–9, achievement trophies, streak 🔥 and shield 🛡 are product elements, not game elements.
- The app is dark-only and says so to the browser: `<meta name="color-scheme" content="dark">` in `index.html` and `:root { color-scheme: dark }` in `src/index.css`, with `body` text `zinc-100`. Inputs carry no text-colour class; they inherit, and `-webkit-text-fill-color: currentColor` plus an autofill override keep typed text light, while `::placeholder` stays `zinc-500` so a blank field never looks filled. Without the declared scheme, Android drew autofilled and date/month input text in the light scheme's black — the profile fields were unreadable on a phone until 2026-09-14.
- Text stays real text; all copy is verbatim Korean UI copy. Proposed alternatives are marked `[제안]` in design canvases.
- Invariants for any redesign: the **seven-tab structure** (2026-09-17, up from six — the user's own decision to add `업무`; six was itself up from five on 2026-09-16, the addition of `미팅`; five was down from six on 2026-09-15, the removal of `성장`; see [decision log](design-docs/decision-log.md)) and per-screen information items; colour-role mapping; five grade colours; pace and "목표 기여 없음" always visible; evidence-first flows (no completion before attachment); the squared role-model bar whose upper segments are wider; a role stage's headline percentage is always a second, smaller-labelled figure beside the proximity percentage, never merged into it (2026-09-18 — [Rule 14](design-docs/core-beliefs.md#rule-14)); the stage-completion overlay states a fact computed from records, with no portrait, trophy or game visual (user decision 1, 2026-09-18); mono numbers; dark single theme. Every nav label sits on one line at 390 px (`<span className="whitespace-nowrap leading-4">`) — seven labels at `grid-cols-7` narrows each cell to about 51 px (390 − 24 `inset-x-3` − 8 `px-1`, ÷ 7), which is why the label reads `업무` rather than `오늘 업무` (four glyphs plus a space would clip at that width); this is checked, not assumed.
